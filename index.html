            ◆ Guidebook

            ◆ Welcome to your bookshelf

            This shelf comes filled with someone else's books, and the pins those books earned, so you can see how everything works.

            Ready to make it yours? Clear them out and start with an empty shelf and an empty pin board.

            You can still do this later in Settings → Start over.

            ◆ How Backups Work

            Where your books live

            There's no account and no server. Your shelf is saved inside this browser on this device, and nowhere else.

            That means a different browser, another computer, your phone or a private window each start with their own empty shelf. Clearing your browser's site data will also wipe it.

            Export: save a copy

            Export Library (Backup) downloads one file, named bookshelf-backup- plus today's date, to your Downloads folder.

            It holds everything: every book with its cover, notes and dates, your custom genres, and your settings (theme, shelf name, goals, fonts, your critter…).

            Keep it somewhere safe, like a cloud drive or email it to yourself. Exporting never changes your shelf.

            Import: bring a copy back

            Import Library asks for a backup file, then replaces the shelf here with what's in it. Books on this shelf that aren't in the file are gone, so export first if you're unsure.

            Use it to restore after something went wrong, or to move your shelf to a new browser or device: export on the old one, import on the new one.

            Reminders

            A note pops up if you haven't backed up in a while (a week at first, then a month after your last export), and the Export button glows now and then. Turn this off under Settings → Backups.

            ◆ Shelf Photo

            One photo of a row of spines, split into a spine photo for each book. The app guesses where each spine starts and ends; you fix the lines, then say which book each one is.

            What's in your photo?

            For the best guesses: shoot straight on, square to the spines rather than from an angle, in good even light (no glare, and no strong shadows across the books). On a shelf, stand the books straight up and down, not leaning; in a stack, keep them lying flat.

            ...or paste a picture anywhere in this window.
